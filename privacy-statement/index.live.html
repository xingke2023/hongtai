  • use our e-Billing services or manage your online account and investment options through the Online Services;
  • use Online Service tools, calculators, and other resources that request your, your family’s, or your beneficiary’s information;
  • access other applications and services from the Online Services, such as when you apply for a job or use the Locate an Agent function;
  • subscribe to newsletters or other communications from us;
  • provide feedback, request information, or otherwise contact us through the Online Services;
  • participate in social media platforms where WFG maintains a presence, such as Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn, or when you make information available to us and others via social media or via forums, blogs, email lists, chat rooms, or other similar technologies;

We may collect the following information, including as reasonably required to complete a request, provide a Product, support your efforts to obtain a Product from companies or insurers we may represent, or complete a transaction on your behalf: first and last name, home or other physical address, telephone number, email address, other identifiers that permit an individual to be contacted physically or online, date of birth, Product numbers, data about transactions with us or those we represent (such as account balances, coverages and benefits, and payment and claims history), education information, professional information, employment information, medical and health information, banking information, credit card number and other financial information, government identification (such as social security or other tax identification, driver’s license, or passport numbers), age, race, disability status, criminal history, marital status, gender, security credentials (such as passwords or voiceprints), and other information.

When you use mobile devices to access our websites, we may receive information about your location and your mobile device. Information collected automatically, including through Data Technologies, may include: your IP address and associated activity tracking; the type of browser and operating system you use; the date and time you access the Online Services; the address of websites you visit prior to or after visiting our Online Services; the number of times you access our Online Services; the pages viewed and features you access during your visit to our Online Services; responses to advertisements and promotions on the Online Services, other websites, and mobile applications; BOI emails you open and links you click within those emails; data regarding your internet service provider or mobile carrier geolocation data (to the extent provided by you or your device); inferences which we may gather relating to your preferences, attitudes, characteristics, or behaviors; and the type of handheld or mobile device that you use.

Notice of Monitoring of Services.  We and our third-party vendors and partners may monitor visits to our websites and mobile applications, including sessions of users.  This monitoring may log the details of your visits to our websites and mobile applications information, such as clicks, page visits, text entered, how long you spent on a page, what you viewed on a page, information you submit, communications and other details of your visits. The Data Technology described above (see “Information Collected Automatically”) may be used to collect and retain usage data for the purposes described in this Statement, including for marketing, analytics, and security purposes and to improve your experience on our websites and mobile applications.

This can include any interaction between you and our chat technologies, information you input into chats, and the conversations you have through our chat functionalities.
